# Law

This is a labelling lab. It is not God Tier Metal.

- Roles: intro, build, riff, hook, breakdown, solo, chill, pulse, outro.
- Human boxes beat Guess, GP markers, allin1, madmom.
- Overlap different roles. Do not stack the same role on the same seconds.
- Extract only rows where the GP5 is that song.
- GP7 is not usable. Partial GP5 is marked gold in the list.
- Do not train a song model on raw FLACs.
- Do not put FLACs, WAVs, Guitar Pro, or tokens in git.
- Do not scrape tabs.
- When 1222 riff extract is importable, use it. Do not grow a second parser.

Default: venv → `boo-lab studio` → label green GP5 tracks first.
